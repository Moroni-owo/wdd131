Top Places to Visit in Machu Picchu

Here are the most iconic locations you shouldn't miss when visiting Machu Picchu:

1. The Sun Gate (Inti Punku)

This viewpoint offers one of the most breathtaking panoramas of Machu Picchu. It is also the traditional entrance for those hiking the Inca Trail.

2. Temple of the Sun

Known for its beautiful stonework and ceremonial significance, this temple was used by the Incas to honor the Sun god.

3. The Inca Bridge

A hidden and adventurous trail leading to a stone bridge carved into the cliffs. It was used as a secret entrance and defensive structure.

4. Huayna Picchu Mountain

The iconic peak behind the ruins. The hike is steep but rewards visitors with stunning aerial views of Machu Picchu.
