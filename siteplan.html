Site Plan – Machu Picchu Travel Guide

Site Name

Machu Picchu Explorer

I chose this name because it reflects the purpose of the website: helping travelers explore, plan, and prepare for their visit to Machu Picchu.

Optional domain: machu-explorer.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide a complete visitor guide to Machu Picchu. The site will include ticket information, travel routes, visitor tips, itineraries, weather details, schedules, and essential recommendations for a safe, organized visit.

Scenarios

  • What type of ticket do I need to buy and where can I purchase it?
  • What is the best way to travel from Cusco to Machu Picchu?
  • What essential items should I pack for the trip?

Color Scheme
